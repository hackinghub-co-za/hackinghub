WHO IS HACKING HUB FOR?
Hacking Hub is a private career accelerator community built for ambitious individuals pursuing a career in cybersecurity. Whether you are transitioning from another field, a recent graduate, a student, or a junior engineer, we provide the daily accountability, mentorship, and resources needed to remain consistent and become job-ready.
WHAT IS THE ACCOUNTABILITY CHALLENGE?
It is our structured quarterly operation. Operatives set specific, daily technical goals (such as completing TryHackMe rooms, coding, or writing technical blogs) and check in daily to verify progress. Operatives who maintain consistency are eligible to win the ultimate bounty, such as free certification exam vouchers.
DO I NEED PRIOR CYBERSECURITY EXPERIENCE TO JOIN?
No. We welcome operatives at all levels, from complete beginners to intermediate security professionals. Our community focuses on building discipline, hands-on skills, and sharing knowledge. We help you decrypt the landscape and construct a clear, customized career roadmap.
HOW DO THE 1-ON-1 STRATEGY SESSIONS WORK?
As a Monthly, Permanent, or Elite member, you get direct access to book interactive 1-on-1 calls. During these sessions, we audit your resume/CV and LinkedIn presence, design a custom study roadmap, run technical/behavioral mock interviews, and discuss direct strategies to land your first cybersecurity job. Basic Access members receive resume/CV and LinkedIn profile reviews asynchronously, but do not have access to interactive 1-on-1 strategy sessions or custom roadmaps.
HOW DO THE EXAM BENEFITS WORK?
Monthly and Permanent members receive standard CompTIA exam voucher discounts. For high-performing Permanent members who hit milestone objectives within our accountability program, we cover Azure exam costs to validate cloud competence. Elite members have all certification fees, course costs, and training fully sponsored by Hacking Hub. The Basic Access tier does not include CompTIA discounts or Azure exam coverage.
CAN I CANCEL MY MEMBERSHIP ANYTIME?
Yes. The Basic Access, Monthly Operative, and Elite Operative tiers run on rolling monthly subscriptions with no contracts. You can cancel at any point without penalty. The Permanent Access tier is a fixed 6-month installment plan that grants lifetime access.
